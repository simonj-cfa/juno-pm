# Human Evaluation Rubric · Juno

_Module 5. Generated by `M5 - Human Evaluation Rubric.html`. Paste over `05-evals/human-rubric.md`._

## What graders score

- **What you’re scoring:** Customer insight quality for daily report
- **Value proposition:** Juno's role is to turn raw customer insights and data into a daily, cumulative insights report, delivered to me each day. This rubric will define what constitutes a high-quality insight from customer data; another rubric is required for holistic report quality.

## Dimensions

| Dimension | Question | Scale | 1 or No | 3 | 5 or Yes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Insight Accuracy | Is the insight presented by Juno an accurate synthesis of real customer data? | 1 to 5 | Not at all - the insight isn't meaningfully connected to the customer feedback source from which it originated. | Somewhat - the insight is directionally accurate to the originating customer feedback source, but imprecise or generic. | Yes - the insight is specific, and clearly and accurately based on the originating customer feedback source. |
| Genuine Origin (deal-breaker) | Is this insight based on real customer feedback, with one or more actual, dated source of feedback? | Yes / No | No - there is no real, validated source of customer feedback informing the insight. | n/a | Yes - there is at least one real, validated source of customer feedback informing the insight. |
| Uniqueness of Insight | Is this insight new, or has it been captured before? | Yes / No | No - this insight is not unique. The insight and its accompanying data source have been previously reported. | n/a | Yes - this insight is unique to this daily report; neither the insight or its accompany customer feedback source has been previously recorded. |

## Calibration

- **How many answers you’ll score, and how often:** We'll score each insight on three consecutive daily reports.
- **Who scores them with you:** Me and the other PM weekly
- **What happens when two of you disagree:** We will discuss all individual scoring disagreements.

## Pass bar

4 or higher on Insight Accuracy; Yes on all other criteria.

**Fails outright, whatever the average:**

- Genuine Origin: any "no"
- Hallucinations, incorrect sources, or incorrect alignment fail automatically.
