## What it demonstrates

## Debrief

- **What worked:** Holistic view of high-level themes and issues with multiple sources, which is what I was looking for. 
- **What broke / felt like a toy:** Likely too much information on a single screen - need to get crisper on what the core dashboard should look like. Also, difficult to easily check evidence sources.
- **What I'd change next pass:** Upload some actual sample data so it feels more relevant for me personally.
